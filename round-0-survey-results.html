Methods and notes

Analysis base

Thirty-eight submissions were received from 37 unique respondents. The latest response was retained for one repeated respondent identifier. All 37 respondents included in the analysis consented to aggregate use of their responses. Analysis of all 38 submissions produced materially similar findings; item percentages changed by no more than about 2.2 percentage points.

Denominators

Most results use n=37. Multiple-response percentages can sum to more than 100%. Q7 N/A ratings were excluded from the corresponding item mean and High/Essential percentage, giving valid denominators of 35–37. The planning item has responses only for the 10 submissions received 6–13 August; earlier exported records are blank for this item, so its percentages use n=10.

Response rules

Five Q4 submissions contained six or seven selections despite the requested five-option cap; all submitted selections were retained. Two respondents selected two rather than three Q7 first-phase priorities.

Other selections

“Other” was selected by one respondent in Q11, one in Q12, and three in Q13. The export contains checkbox selections but no accompanying write-in field or text, so these responses cannot be further categorized.

Interpretation

  • This is a descriptive, self-selected, non-probability sample; no inferential or representativeness claims are made.
  • No subgroup filters are shown because small groups could produce unstable comparisons or privacy concerns.
  • The export did not specify a timezone for collection timestamps.

Open-text responses

All 11 nonblank Q6 responses, 7 substantive Q8 responses, 27 substantive Q14 responses, 9 nonblank Q15 responses, and 11 substantive Q16 responses are shown above without attribution and in non-submission order. Twenty-six respondents left Q16 blank.
